I am a B.Sc. (Hons) Geography student dedicated to bridging the gap between Earth Science and Software Engineering. My academic focus spans Physical Geography, Hydrology, and Climatology, where I apply a research-driven mindset to analyze complex spatial patterns and environmental data.
